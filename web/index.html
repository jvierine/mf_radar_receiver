Power, Doppler + winds · latest 15 minutes

Realtime signal and fading-wind analysis

—

Both panels use the same 15-minute window, 10-second cadence, and 0–300 km range grid. Every time–range cell is retained. The upper panel is the sum of independently fitted narrowband power from the three dipoles divided by processed broadband noise power, shown on −20…20 dB. Broadband noise is mean 30–50 km power after transmit-phase correction, 20 kHz filtering, 10× decimation, and coherent integration of five 10 ms IPPs. The lower panel fits one common Doppler frequency while allowing an independent complex amplitude on each dipole. Monostatic velocity uses vr = −λfD/2. No SNR, coherence, AoA, altitude, zenith-angle, or velocity gate is applied. The loop is excluded because its lower SNR and stronger RFI can degrade the fit. The bottom panels estimate zonal and meridional neutral winds at 70–120 km altitude from five-minute, three-dipole power-fading correlations. An evolving elliptical diffraction pattern is fitted automatically; its ground speed is divided by two. In the near-vertical thin-layer Fresnel approximation, the two-way path depends on scatterer and receiver coordinates as (X−x/2)²/h, so a scatterer displacement produces twice that displacement in the ground pattern. Estimates require cross-correlation ≥0.15 and correlation-model RMSE ≤0.35. Blank wind cells failed these objective quality tests. Velocity color scales are fixed at −150…+150 m/s.

Quick-look method

From voltage to noise-referenced power

  1. Correct the complex voltage. The known USRP digital-downconverter offset, −0.25−0.25i, is removed from every sample.
  2. Reference each transmitted pulse. The phase of the first 100 samples of the direct-path transmit pulse on channel 1 is measured and removed from all three dipole voltages.
  3. Filter and coherently integrate. A 50-tap Hann-windowed 20 kHz low-pass filter is applied, the voltage is decimated from 1 MHz to 100 kHz, and five consecutive 10 ms IPPs are added coherently. For a phase-stable echo, five-pulse integration provides a nominal factor-five (7.0 dB) SNR gain.
  4. Combine the dipoles. Power is formed after coherent integration and averaged across channels 1, 3, and 4. The broadband loop channel is excluded. The resulting round-trip range-gate spacing is approximately 1.5 km.
  5. Estimate the noise floor. For each one-minute profile, the 20th percentile of power between 250 and 1400 km is used as a robust receiver-noise estimate.
  6. Reference power to the background. The displayed value is 10 log₁₀(P/Pbackground). This noise-biased power ratio is deliberately not labelled physical SNR: noise fluctuates around 0 dB and can legitimately plot below it. The mesospheric view is fixed at −3 to 20 dB; the full-range view is fixed at −3 to 35 dB.

One profile is retained per minute over a rolling 48-hour window. This is an operational quick-look. It uses coherent pulse integration, but not phase calibration between antennas, beamforming, or Doppler selection. The synchronized ten-second product above adds only the ungated common-frequency Doppler fit.
